Local-First Privacy, Storage Audit & Algorithmic Conventions

Verified Mathematical & Numerical Conventions
  • Zero Runtime Dependencies: Special functions (Lanczos log-gamma, Lentz continued-fraction beta/gamma, Acklam inverse normal, Cornish-Fisher + Newton-Raphson Student-t), Durbin-Levinson PACF, LOESS STL, and Nelder-Mead run locally in ES2022.
  • Sample Variance & Quantiles: Unbiased sample variance (n − 1) and Hyndman–Fan Type 7 quantiles.
  • Strict Temporal Causality: Transformations, imputations, and MASE denominators are computed strictly inside each training origin window.
